Why this exists

Since 8 October 2026 an XRPL account can give another account power to send transactions as it. That is useful for businesses — and dangerous if a scam site tricks you into granting it.

What it never does

It never asks for your secret key or recovery words, never holds funds, and has no server. Your wallet signs; this page only reads the public ledger. (The Testnet Practice Lab signs only with throwaway Testnet keys it gets from the faucet.)
